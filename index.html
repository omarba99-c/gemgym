Protein & Muscle Preservation Guide

Adequate protein intake combined with progressive resistance training signals muscle retention during a calorie deficit, increases thermic effect of feeding, and improves meal satiety.

Evidence-Based Nutrition Principles

1. Energy Balance & Sustainable Deficit Fat loss occurs when average energy expenditure exceeds intake over weeks—moderate deficits (300–500 kcal) prevent metabolic fatigue and preserve gym performance.
2. Why Spot Reduction Is Impossible Abdominal crunches and torso machines strengthen and hypertrophy core musculature, but fat mobilization occurs systemically across the entire body through a sustained calorie deficit.
3. Fiber, Volume & Hunger Management Aim for 28–38g of dietary fiber daily from legumes (lupini beans, lentils), vegetables, oats, and whole fruits to slow gastric emptying and stabilize blood glucose.
4. Portion Control & Consistency Over Perfection Reassess calorie targets using 7-day rolling weight averages rather than single-day scale shifts.
